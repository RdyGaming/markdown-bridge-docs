Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Short version: Markdown Bridge does not collect, store or send your content or personal data anywhere. It has no server.

Who we are

Markdown Bridge for Confluence ("the app") is provided by RedHead ("we", "us"). Contact: doustd+redheaddev@gmail.com.

What the app processes

When you use the app, it reads and writes Confluence content on your behalf: page titles, page bodies, labels, attachments, and the names and keys of spaces you can see. All of this happens between your browser and Atlassian's own Confluence API, using your signed-in session and your own permissions.

  • We do not receive this content. The app has no backend service operated by us.
  • The app does not store content, settings or logs in any database, including Atlassian-hosted app storage.
  • The app makes no network requests to any host other than Atlassian.
  • The app uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or advertising.
  • Files you choose for import are read in your browser and sent only to your Confluence site.
  • Exported files are built in your browser and saved to your device.

Personal data in your content

Your pages may contain personal data, for example names in @mentions. The app converts that content as part of an import or export you start. It stays within your browser and Atlassian. You remain the controller of that data. Because we never receive it, we do not act as a processor of it.

What we receive from Atlassian

As a Marketplace vendor we receive licensing and sales information from Atlassian about organizations that install the app, such as the site name, license type, user tier and a technical and billing contact. Atlassian provides this under its own privacy policy and Marketplace terms. We use it only to provide support, meet tax and accounting duties, and understand how many sites use the app. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Support requests

If you contact support, we receive what you send us: your email address and the details in your message. Please do not send confidential content. We keep support messages for up to 24 months and then delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers needed to answer support email, and when the law requires it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal data we hold about you, such as support messages. Write to doustd+redheaddev@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. For data held by Atlassian, contact Atlassian or your site administrator.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Data residency and transfers

The app runs on Atlassian Forge with no outbound data flow, so your content stays wherever Atlassian hosts your Confluence site.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Marketplace release notes.
